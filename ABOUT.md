# About BetterSantaCruz

BetterSantaCruz is an independent community project exploring how a small, accessible, source-led civic portal could serve residents of Santa Cruz, Laguna. It is not the official Municipality of Santa Cruz website, a government system, or an endorsement by any public office.

## Why it exists

Public information can be difficult to locate, compare, and understand on a phone. This project focuses on navigation, provenance, readable empty states, and a contribution process that makes corrections easier without pretending that incomplete research is complete civic data.

## How it is built

The foundation preserves the BetterLB/BetterGov ecosystem shape: React 19, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Kapwa semantic tokens, i18n, Cloudflare Pages/D1 seams, source-aware OpenLGU pipeline utilities, and browser tests. The architecture is retained for compatibility; Los Baños factual content and branding are not part of the Santa Cruz data model.

## Evidence first

The source ledger distinguishes verified observations, restricted pages, unreachable leads, discovery-only references, secondary context, and collaboration records. Important local claims remain unpublished until their municipality identity, freshness, and provenance are reviewed.

## Current scope

The current implementation is a foundation: source registry, data-policy documentation, identity safeguards, and honest empty states. Officials, barangays, services, legislation, finance, infrastructure, tourism, emergency contacts, and statistics are research gaps rather than invented content.

## Governance

Santa Cruz registration is handled separately through BetterLGU upstream PR #244 by @Diannn3. Pagsanjan remains the existing @rswlljms effort; BetterSantaCruz will only prepare an ethical collaboration handoff.
